about

A little about how I work

I'm an aspiring Machine Learning and AI Engineer currently studying Computer Science & Engineering at AIUB, with a growing focus on LLM engineering, Retrieval-Augmented Generation, and agentic AI systems. I like problems that sit between research and engineering — reading a paper on Tuesday and having a benchmark for it running by Friday.

My recent work spans the full ML systems stack: fine-tuning and serving models efficiently with vLLM, building agentic RAG pipelines with multi-hop retrieval and re-ranking, and compressing models with quantization and distillation so they're actually cheap to run.

Alongside coursework, I'm writing two research papers — one benchmarking classical ML against deep learning, and one on LLM-serving performance — plus an undergraduate thesis extending that work. I'm also an Executive at the AIUB Research & Development Club.
